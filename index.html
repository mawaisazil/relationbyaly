Built after years of working with couples who kept hitting the same walls. Each letter is a skill you build. Each one makes the next one easier.

Tune into your triggers

Recognize what's happening in your body before a conversation escalates — so you can pause instead of react.

Assess your assumptions

Catch the stories your brain writes under stress, and learn to replace them with curiosity instead of certainty.

Before you enroll

Is this therapy?

No. TALK Like You Love Each Other is an educational course built on therapeutic principles by a licensed therapist, but it's a practical skills course, not a substitute for therapy. If you're in crisis or dealing with abuse, please reach out to a licensed professional directly.

How long does the course take?

Each module runs 1–2 hours including video, journaling, and reflection. It's self-paced with lifetime access, but most students find one module a week feels right.

What if I've tried other things and they haven't worked?

Most communication advice is either too vague or too clinical. TALK gives you a specific, step-by-step framework — with scripts, exercises, and tools for real conversations, not just concepts.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes — a 14-day money-back guarantee. Complete the course, and if it wasn't helpful, a short note gets you a full refund. No hoops, no hard feelings.
